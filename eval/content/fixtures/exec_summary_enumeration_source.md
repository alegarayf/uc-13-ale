## Elder Care Homecare — Investment Committee Diligence Executive Summary

**Business Overview**
Elder Care Homecare is a private-pay home care company operating across six locations in the Tri-State region (NY, NJ, CT, and MA), providing home health aide, live-in, and nursing services to elderly patients. The company generates revenue by billing private-pay clients for caregiver hours delivered, with approximately 352 active clients and 2,123 registered caregivers across markets including NYC, Long Island, Westchester, NJ, MA, and CT as of Q2 2025. The platform has grown both organically and through acquisition, having completed at least two transactions — Guided Living and Unicity — within the recent historical window, and is currently ramping a Connecticut de novo location. Pro Forma Adjusted Revenue reached $46.4M on a TTM Aug-24 basis per the Historical P&L Summary workstream.

**Financial Picture and Quality of Earnings**
Revenue has grown substantially from $8,955K in 2020A to $46,423K TTM Aug-24, with Pro Forma Adjusted EBITDA of $9,239K representing a 19.9% margin on a pro forma basis. However, the Quality of Earnings workstream flags that reported (pre-addback) EBITDA margin is approximately 7.9% — below the ~10% healthcare services threshold — and that the addback schedule contains 17 discrete Tier 4 items totaling approximately $7.3M in gross adjustments against a reported EBITDA base of roughly $7.7M, representing approximately 247% of reported EBITDA. Every addback item has been classified Tier 4 because no supporting documents are referenced in the addback schedule within the VDR. The five largest individual addbacks — run-rate executive compensation ($2,490K), Unicity pre-acquisition results ($1,077K), Unicity synergies ($909K), a cash-to-accrual revenue adjustment ($665K), and the Guided Living ramp-up adjustment ($430K) — each individually exceed 5% of reported EBITDA and include forward-looking pro forma synergies and pre-close acquisition earnings that have not been validated against audited standalone financials or demonstrated post-close performance. The reconciliation workstream flagged one mismatch and was unable to verify nine items. If even a portion of these addbacks are rejected in a buyer QofE, the defensible EBITDA base could approach zero or turn negative, fundamentally altering valuation and leverage capacity. The forecast projects revenue growing from $47.2M (2024E) to $197.8M (2029P) — a 4.2x increase — driven by organic growth, the Connecticut de novo ramp, and additional expansion; the Forecast workstream rates this Red given the aggressive assumptions embedded in that trajectory.

**Top Risks and Red Flags**
The diligence process has surfaced several critical and material issues across workstreams. On compliance and regulatory risk (KPI workstream), the company received adverse NYSDOH survey citations in May 2023 related to HCR Profile maintenance, background-check consent procedures, and Home Care Registry credentialing timelines; written DOH confirmation of closure, any corrective action plans, and the absence of ongoing monitoring obligations have not been confirmed. Separately, a diligence request item flags the need to verify that no agreements exist with OIG/SAM-excluded individuals or entities — a potential False Claims Act exposure that has not been cleared. On financial quality (Quality of Earnings and Financial Trends workstreams), the addback stack's composition — including speculative forward synergies and unaudited pre-close acquisition earnings — represents the single most consequential open item for price and structure. On legal and customer risk (Legal Contracts and Customer Quality workstreams), outside counsel was retained as recently as April 30, 2025 to pursue collection of patient receivables, raising questions about billing integrity and working capital quality; material customer contracts contain termination-for-convenience provisions whose interaction with a change-of-control has not been confirmed; and two identified clients show significant multi-year billing declines whose treatment in the run-rate revenue base has not been clarified. The Manhattan office lease contains an anti-assignment covenant that may require landlord consent as a closing condition, and unusual indemnity provisions appear across multiple contracts including the Unicity Asset Purchase Agreement.

**Confidence and Key Open Items**
Overall analytical confidence across all seven workstreams is rated Medium, and five of seven workstreams carry a Red rating (Financial Trends, KPI, Legal Contracts, Quality of Earnings, and Forecast), with the remaining two rated Yellow (Business Model, Customer Quality). The most consequential open items prior to any pricing or structuring decision are: (1) seller-provided audited or third-party-verified support for each of the 17 Tier 4 addbacks, with particular focus on the five items individually exceeding 5% of EBITDA; (2) written DOH confirmation of regulatory citation closure and a completed OIG/SAM exclusion screening for all employees, contractors, and vendors; (3) quantification and aging of receivables in active legal collection; (4) confirmation of whether material customer contracts contain change-of-control triggers; and (5) production of location-level P&L and operational KPIs for Guided Living, Unicity, ECHC, and the Connecticut de novo to validate the entity-level EBITDA build-up. The Legal Contracts workstream should also engage outside counsel to confirm whether the Manhattan lease anti-assignment provision applies to the contemplated transaction structure.
