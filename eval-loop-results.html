What worked, and what we stopped doing

Kept moving the number

  • Company-scoped query / filter in the harness — leftover gold into the returned pool without rewriting shared registry strings.
  • Small-bag gold identity — replace destack clones with in-pool right-class chunk ids (Northbound contracts 0→0.692; Sherpa D21).
  • Judge XOR with a confined bind — 026 / 011 / 043+051 / 048. Agreement 32/53 → 44/53. Sample-wide prompt edits were reverted when they collateral-damaged the score.
  • Seating W3 — KPI citation aliases, then first pins, then named leftover overlays. Coverage win, not a stretch-30 win.
  • Parser completeness — never-parsed docs and missing docx/xlsx deps. Real ingest; exam mean often held or dipped.

Tried, measured, retired

  • Leftover-zero ranking when gold is not in the pool — vacuous gate_pass, then revert. Now a written dead rule.
  • Destack to shrink the bag — gated set shrinks; do not treat a smaller exam as a higher score.
  • Shared BMA / KPI string rewrites — collide across companies. Company-only overrides only.
  • Bonus-term chasing (payroll-build, clinic, article7, corp-org) — gold still out of pool.
  • Force-reparse filtered residuals and bagging huge destack leftovers — not a 1-point lever.
