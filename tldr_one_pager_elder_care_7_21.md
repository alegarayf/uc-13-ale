## Business Snapshot

Elder Care is a multi-market home care services platform operating across six locations in New York (NYC, Westchester, Long Island), New Jersey, Massachusetts, and Connecticut. The company delivers Home Health Aide (hourly), Live-In Care (daily), and RN Nursing (hourly) services to approximately 595 clients as of Q4-24E, with a recurring, high-intensity care model averaging 60 billed hours per week per client. Revenue has grown at a ~30% CAGR from 2020 to TTM Aug-24, reaching $46.4M on a pro forma adjusted basis, driven by both organic expansion in legacy New York markets and acquisitions of Unicity (NJ) and Guided Living (MA). New locations (CT, MA, NJ) contributed $12.4M of TTM Aug-24 revenue versus $3.9M in 2023, reflecting rapid ramp. Gross margins have held in the 41–44% range across the period. The workforce comprises 2,123 active caregivers company-wide as of April 2025, with W-2 employment and credentialing standards enforced across acquired subsidiaries. Payer mix includes private pay, Medicaid Waiver (~11%), VA programs (~7%), and LTC insurance (~11%), introducing episodic reimbursement exposure alongside the private-pay core.

## Risk Mitigation

**Structural and Operational Offsets:** The W-2 employment model across all subsidiaries (including Unicity and Guided Living) reduces worker misclassification exposure and supports regulatory compliance posture. Credentialing standards (CHHA/CNA/HHA minimum, one year experience at Guided Living) provide a quality floor. The weekly KPI dashboard — tracking census, caregiver hires, missed clock-ins, on-call events, and DPS complaints by market — gives management real-time operational visibility that is above average for a business of this size. **Management Actions Required:** CoC landlord consent on Long Island and Manhattan leases must be obtained pre-close; failure to do so is a deal-stopper. The outside counsel engagement for patient receivables collection (Peter Ackerman Esq.) should be scoped and quantified — the AR aging by payor (currently absent) is the key document. **Diligence Follow-Ups That Could Partially Mitigate QofE Risk:** An independent QofE with access to subsidiary general ledgers, payroll records, and acquisition closing statements could validate or recast the $4,945K addback pool. Audited or reviewed financials for Guided Living and Unicity pre-acquisition periods would directly address the $1,454K of pre-close earnings in the bridge. AR aging by payor would resolve the $665K cash-to-accrual adjustment. Caregiver utilization root-cause analysis by market (demand gap vs. scheduling vs. onboarding lag) would contextualize the workforce efficiency concern. None of these mitigants are currently available in the data room.
