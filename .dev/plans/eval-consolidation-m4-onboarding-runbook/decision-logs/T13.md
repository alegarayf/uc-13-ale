Explicitly **not** granted: `kpi_claim_intent_map.yaml`, `clearsulting.yaml`, `GoldLabel` schema, `exclude_reason` vocabulary.

## Chosen approach

1. Reshape `gold_exclusions.yaml` to `companies: {<canonical_slug>: {excluded: [...]}}` with folded slug keys (`elder_care`, `clearsulting`).
2. Require keyword-only `company_slug` on `load_gold_exclusions`; unknown slug → `{}`; malformed top-level → `PreconditionError`.
3. Cache exclusions in `GoldLabelBootstrap` keyed on `canonical_company_slug(self.company_name)`.
4. Re-express disjointness **per company**: each company's excluded KPI intents must be disjoint from KPI intents that company's **committed gold** shows as claim-resolved (`ready`/`partial`, not `aggregate_exclude`).
5. Restore Elder Care's three annotations byte-compatible with pre-`0a5b2f2`; close T11 debt row; add GAP-103 evidence line (population 5).

**This is not a revert of `0a5b2f2`.** Clearsulting's claim-map additions and live claim targets stay. Only Elder Care's annotations return, now legally, because exclusions are company-scoped.
