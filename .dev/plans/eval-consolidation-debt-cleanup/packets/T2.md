
**Gap 1 (`rung`).** When `attestation in {"attested","partial"}` **and** `layer != "content_correctness"`, a non-null `rung` is accepted silently. Your new branch belongs **inside** the `if row.attestation in {"attested","partial"}:` block, as the `else`/negative case of the `layer == "content_correctness"` test.

**Gap 2 (`method`).** `method` is validated for vocabulary membership only. There is no check that it is null outside `ingest_completeness`.
